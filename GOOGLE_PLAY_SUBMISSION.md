Its listing says that users configure Android’s own Digital Wellbeing controls themselves.

### Data Safety position

Complete the form from the final AAB and observed SDK traffic. The present source has no analytics/network SDK, account, or sensitive permission. Re-audit transitive dependencies and the merged manifest before answering “no data collected.”

### Listing review

Reject “Block distractions for exams,” “app blocker,” “system shield,” “hard blocker,” “locks apps,” “unbreakable,” iOS screenshots, and any parity claim. Show the manual instructions and Settings handoff exactly as shipped.
